The Sweet Origin of Ice Cream

A journey through time to discover how our favorite frozen treat was born.

Ancient Beginnings

The origins of frozen desserts date back to early China (around 200 BC) and Roman Emperor Nero, who flavored mountain snow with fruits.

The Persian Innovation

Persians invented chilled rosewater and vermicelli dishes, utilizing underground structures called *Yakhchals* to store ice in the desert.

Evolution in Europe

Marco Polo introduced sherbet-like recipes to Italy, which later spread to the French royal court via Catherine de' Medici in 1553.

The Modern Era

Commercial availability began in late 17th-century Paris, leading to mass production and global ice cream culture in the 19th century.
